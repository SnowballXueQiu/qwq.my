The room was stuffy in the autumn heat. I picked up a fountain pen that hadn't touched ink in ages, feeling I ought to write something, yet not knowing where to begin; the weather, most likely, had left me restless. I wrote your name in a crooked hand and left it at that. Sorry there are so few sweet words, but I spent a whole half hour thinking of you.
