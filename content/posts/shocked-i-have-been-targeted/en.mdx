Something recently happened that left me not knowing whether to laugh or cry. A while ago, with nothing better to do, I wrote a joke project called **`sb.h`**:

https://github.com/SnowballXueQiu/sb.h

It was purely for fun, and I never expected it to take off in a certain small circle. Honestly, I didn't think much of it at the time; I figured people were just passing it around for a laugh.

Then a friend suddenly told me he'd seen my project on Xiaohongshu (RedNote).

![](/blog/shocked-i-have-been-targeted/01.webp)

My first reaction was shock: "Since when do I have anything to do with Xiaohongshu?" Curiosity got the better of me, so I downloaded the app to see for myself. Sure enough, the comments were "a hundred flowers in bloom": plenty of people were picking my project apart, and many were simply cursing. Looking at the screen, I couldn't help laughing out loud: is this thing really worth getting so worked up about?

And that wasn't the end of it. I then found out that my project had been reposted on X by **[hsn](https://x.com/hsn8086)** and **[雲鳩](https://x.com/cldeop)**. What is this, meeting your idols? Being reposted by people like them stirred up all sorts of feelings; maybe my joke projects really are a cut above. What surprised me even more was that OSChina (开源中国) reposted it too. To be honest, I was a bit shocked when I saw that, but mostly disappointed: I really wish organisations like that would do something useful, instead of always chasing whatever's trending like **"journalism majors"**.

![](/blog/shocked-i-have-been-targeted/02.webp)

At heart it's a joke project, just something to play with. But the people cursing me in the comments genuinely make me laugh: perhaps they can't beat it on technical grounds, so they try to feel relevant with their mouths. I don't really mind this sort of thing, though. It's all for fun, so why take it seriously?

All in all, the experience showed me how much life a joke project can have, and gave me a taste of "breaking out of my bubble". It's a little exasperating, but also pretty fun. After all, they can curse me all they like; the project did take off.
