### Tired of drifting, you found your way home.

Unfinished, sorrowful poems hide in your dimples; your glassy eyes glint from a quiet corner at the mouth of the alley. A thin moss lies across the cardboard box. Your call, a light feather skimming a lake of damp blue, found me beyond ten thousand noises. From then on, <mark>love</mark> became a tough vine that never stops growing, one end tied to you and the other to me.

![Image 1](/blog/love-unveiled/05.webp)

### Do you remember? We once made a <mark>promise</mark>.

"Wait a little longer. When we've grown up, we'll go and see the faraway places from our textbooks together." You leaned against me, not quite understanding, and mewed. Spring was beautiful; flowers filled half the town, and the haze made for deep, drunken dreams. In the dream I held you by Lake Baikal, and you told me about the dragonflies you'd pounced on in the lonely summer and the ripples you'd stirred up in the icy stream, and so we let the rusty, murky glow of sunset settle all over us. You seemed a little tired; resting on my shoulder, you mumbled that you wanted the red finch at the edge of the sky.

![Image 2](/blog/love-unveiled/06.webp)

### <mark>Comfort</mark> is taking someone's side for no reason at all.

Every time I cry, a sliver of bitterness is peeled from my soul, and what's left is only sweet. Your paw pads are fuzzy and pink; they brush across my tear-filled eyes, and the silt washes away. You can't speak, so beneath the bright moon we hold each other. Love drives away the rainy season. Our <mark>love</mark> will never stand still.

![Image 3](/blog/love-unveiled/07.webp)

### Love lives on, endlessly, in our poems.

We tread across a ground of fallen leaves, a yellowing sea where thirty thousand tides rise and fall in uncertain waves. You stride along with your head held high, and the crunching memories break into the growth rings, deep in the island of the heart, lingering and winding. Autumn has brought its harvest; our love is golden, and breathes like a gardenia.

![Image 4](/blog/love-unveiled/08.webp)

### I give love ten thousand definitions, and every one of them points to you.

Let's wait for the snow together, close under the same scarf. Turn around for no reason, and a whole four seasons have passed. In our hearts there is a house facing the sea, where spring is warm and the flowers bloom. Our eyes meet once more, and tears still glisten at their corners.

![Image 5](/blog/love-unveiled/09.webp)

**Luckily, this time it's joy that shows on our faces. Love is a password we both <mark>understand without a word</mark>.**
